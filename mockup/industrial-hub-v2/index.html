Who we are

From one Binondo storefront to a nationwide distribution network.

Herco Trading began in 1908 as a single hardware store in Binondo, Manila. More than a century later, we remain family-led and hands-on, helping global brands reach traditional trade, modern retail and industrial customers across the country.
